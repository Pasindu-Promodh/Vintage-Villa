# Knuckles Hiking Packages - Per 1 Person

## Back to Nature: An Expedition. Visit the Wilderness of Knuckles

You can choose one of the following hikes:

- **Corberts Gap** - Easy (5 Hours)
- **Heel Oya Trail and Village Visit** - Moderate (7 Hours)
- **Knuckles Five Peaks** - Moderate Difficulty (8 Hours)
- **Knuckles Alugal Lena** - Moderate Difficulty (8 Hours)
- **Knuckles 7 Waterfalls** - Difficult (9 Hours)

*(The above times depend on your speed)*

## Package Includes:

- **Guide for Hiking** (Well-experienced, English-speaking guide)
- **Entrance Ticket**
- **Transport** (Tuk-tuk for Heeloya village visit, Knuckles 7 waterfalls hiking, and Knuckles 5 peaks hiking back to the villa)
- **Leech Socks**
- **Lunch** (Sri Lankan traditional rice and curry or sandwiches)
- **Energizer Food Pack** (Chocolate, cheese, banana, biscuits, peanuts)
- **Refreshing River Bath**
- **Filtered Water**
- **Basic First Aid**
